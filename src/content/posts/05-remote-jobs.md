# Remote Jobs

Remote opportunities are available across technology, customer support, sales, design, operations and other fields.

## Check the employer

Look for a verifiable company website, clear job description, realistic requirements and an application process that does not require suspicious payments.

## Protect your information

Do not share passwords, banking credentials or sensitive identity information with an unverified recruiter. Verify important details through official channels.
