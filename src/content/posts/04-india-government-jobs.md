# Government Jobs in India

Government recruitment notices can contain important information about eligibility, vacancies, application dates, fees and examination schedules.

## Check the official notification

Always read the original recruitment notification and verify the recruiting organization's official website before applying or paying any fee.

## Useful information to compare

Check the number of vacancies, educational qualification, age limits, application deadline, selection process and official application link.
