# Software Developer Jobs

Software development covers a wide range of roles, from frontend and backend development to mobile, cloud and platform engineering.

## Skills employers may request

Depending on the role, job descriptions may mention JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Java, SQL, Git, APIs, cloud platforms or specific frameworks.

## Finding suitable roles

Search by job title, technology, seniority and location. Compare the requirements in the vacancy with your current skills before applying.
