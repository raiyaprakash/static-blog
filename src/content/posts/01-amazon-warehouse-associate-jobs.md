# Amazon Warehouse Associate Jobs

Warehouse associate roles are common entry-level opportunities for people looking for work in logistics and fulfilment. Requirements, pay and benefits can vary by location and employer.

## Typical responsibilities

- Receiving and organizing inventory
- Picking, packing and shipping orders
- Following workplace safety procedures
- Using scanners and warehouse equipment
- Maintaining an organized work area

## Common requirements

Many warehouse roles are designed for entry-level applicants. Employers may specify age, work authorization, physical requirements, shift availability or previous experience depending on the position.

## How to apply

Check the employer's official careers page for the current vacancy, location, eligibility requirements and application process. Always review the job description before submitting an application.
