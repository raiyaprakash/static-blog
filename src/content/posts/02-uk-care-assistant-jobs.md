# UK Care Assistant Jobs

Care assistant roles can involve supporting people with daily activities, personal care and wellbeing. Requirements differ between employers and roles.

## What employers may look for

Common requirements can include communication skills, reliability, relevant training and the right to work in the UK. Some roles may require specific qualifications or checks.

## Before applying

Read the full vacancy carefully and confirm the employer, location, working hours, salary information and eligibility requirements.
