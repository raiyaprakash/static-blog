# Career Guide for Job Seekers

A focused job search starts with a clear CV, relevant applications and preparation for interviews.

## Tailor your CV

Highlight skills and experience that directly match the vacancy. Keep the information accurate and easy to scan.

## Prepare for interviews

Review the job description, understand the employer and prepare concise examples that demonstrate your skills and experience.
